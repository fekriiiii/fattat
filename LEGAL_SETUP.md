## Background

Founder is 16. Swedish law allows enskild näringsidkare between 16–18 with two permissions in writing:

1. **Förmyndare (parents)** — soft yes secured 2026-05-15, firm yes 2026-05-16.
2. **Överförmyndaren i kommunen (municipal authority)** — application in progress.

Both permissions must be mailed by post to Bolagsverket. Skatteverket also requires the överförmyndare samtycke for tax registration.

Sources: bolagsverket.se (page on minor sole proprietors), skatteverket.se (page on starting a business as a minor).

---

## The Path — 5 steps

1. **Förmyndare permission in writing** — short signed letter from parent. Status: needs to be drafted/signed once form requirements are known.
2. **Apply for överförmyndare samtycke** — kommun-specific form. Status: requested via email 2026-05-16.
3. **Wait for samtycke decision** — typically 2–6 weeks.
4. **Skatteverket registration** — mandatory. Free. Attach samtycke. Establishes F-skatt.
5. **Bolagsverket name registration** — optional, ~1100 SEK. Reserves "Fattat" as a registered företagsnamn. Defer until cash + brand justify it.

---

## Why enskild firma, not AB

- No 25k aktiekapital required (AB needs this)
- Free to register, free to close
- Light annual reporting (bilaga NE — doable yourself with Bokio)
- Upgrade to AB later if revenue justifies (post-April '27 decision)
- Lower stakes addresses parents' main worry ("what if it flops?")
